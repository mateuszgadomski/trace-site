TRACE

Understand your game.

In development. Not yet open to the public.

Trace is a coaching tool for Counter-Strike 2 players. A player signs in, connects his account, and Trace analyses his own matches to show him his mistakes, his strengths and how his play changes over time.

How match data is used

Trace processes only the matches of a signed-in player who took part in them and connected his account. A demo file is used for one analysis and deleted afterwards. It is never shared or redistributed.
